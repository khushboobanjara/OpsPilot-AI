# Travel and Expense Policy (sample)

## 1. Booking

Flights must be booked at least 14 days ahead through the corporate travel portal. Economy class is required for flights under
6 hours.

## 2. Limits

Hotels are reimbursed up to 200 USD per night in most cities and up to 300 USD per night in New York and San Francisco. Meals are
reimbursed up to 75 USD per day. Alcohol is not reimbursed.

## 3. Claims

Expense claims with receipts must be submitted within 30 days of the trip. Claims above 1,000 USD need manager approval.
