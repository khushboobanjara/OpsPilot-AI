# Vendor Onboarding Procedure (sample)

## 1. Documents required

New vendors must provide a signed W-9 form (or W-8BEN for non-US vendors), a certificate of insurance, and bank account details on
company letterhead before the first payment is released.

## 2. Verification

Procurement verifies the bank account by calling the vendor on a phone number found independently, never one taken from the
invoice or e-mail. The call is logged in the vendor record.

## 3. Review cycle

Vendors are reviewed again every 12 months. Vendors with no invoices for 24 months are deactivated.
