# Accounts Payable Policy (sample)

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets how Northwind Trading pays supplier invoices, who may approve them, and when a person must review an invoice
before it is paid.

## 2. Approval limits

Invoices up to 2,000 USD may be approved by a single reviewer. Invoices above 2,000 USD and up to 10,000 USD need a reviewer and
the finance manager. Invoices above 10,000 USD need two approvers, one of whom is the finance director.

Any invoice from a vendor that has not been paid before, and whose total is above 2,000 USD, must go to human review.

## 3. Payment terms

Standard payment terms are net 30 days from the invoice date. Net 45 is allowed only with written approval from the finance
director. Early payment discounts must be approved by finance before they are taken.

## 4. Duplicate and suspicious invoices

An invoice whose number, vendor and amount match an earlier invoice is a suspected duplicate and must not be paid until a
reviewer has compared both. Changes to a vendor's bank details must be confirmed by phone using a number already on file.
